The Alliance High School Endowment Fund (AHSEF) Quarter 2, 2026 Financial Update is attached for your review. This update summarizes our fund balances, scholarship disbursements, and giving options through the end of Quarter 2, 2026.

### Key Highlights from the Report

Total AHSEF assets stood at **$175,363.73** at the end of Quarter 2, 2026, including **$173,063.73** in endowment funds and **$2,300.00** in the AHSEF Chase operating account. Quarter 2 activity reflects planned scholarship support across the Academic, Need-Based, and Sports funds.

### Q2 2026 Disbursements

A total of **$9,400.00** was disbursed across three funds:

- **$5,000.00** from the Need-Based Scholarship Fund
- **$3,000.00** from the Academic Scholarship Endowment Fund
- **$1,400.00** from the Sports Endowment Fund

### Ways to Give

The report outlines multiple giving options, including:

- Donating online through the [AHSEF donation page](/donate)
- ACH, direct deposit, or wire transfer
- Checks payable to Alliance High School Endowment Fund
- Gifts or transfers of appreciated securities through Vanguard

For current instructions on any of these options, please contact the AHSEF Treasurer at treasurer@ahsef.org.

AHSEF remains committed to prudent stewardship of its funds while continuing to support students and school priorities. We are hopeful that more Old Boys and friends of Alliance High School will strengthen this work through continued contributions, helping expand the Fund's long-term impact for future generations.
